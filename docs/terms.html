What the app is

Recipe Box is a local cookbook: your recipes, photos, kitchen, grocery list, meal plan, and cooking history stay on your device. There is no Recipe Box account.

The Discover library is made of recipes from the Wikibooks Cookbook, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, and photos from Wikimedia Commons under the license shown on each photo. Those licenses stay with that content. Imported recipes from a website you paste are for your personal use and keep a link to the original page.

You own the recipes and photos you add yourself.

Recipe Box Plus

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription at $2.99 per year. There is no free trial.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID through Apple’s In-App Purchase at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your Apple device. Restore purchases is available in the app.

Plus is not yet for sale on the App Store. A test (sandbox) purchase is only possible in a development build, and only after the subscription product exists in App Store Connect. Expo Go and regular App Store builds cannot charge you. Real charges stay off until a production release is approved.

When Plus is for sale, it includes unlimited recipe-link imports (the ongoing perk; the free plan includes 10 a month) and additional fridge-photo and recipe-card scans beyond three free tries each. Card scanning works on all supported iPhones, with smarter reading on Apple Intelligence iPhones (15 Pro and newer, iOS 26+). The fridge helper is a list you confirm. Photos are processed on the device and are never sent to an AI provider. Features that are not built — including live sync between phones — are not part of Plus unless this document and the App Store listing are updated.
