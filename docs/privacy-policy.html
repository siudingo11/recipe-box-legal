Privacy Policy for Recipe Box

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy describes how Recipe Box, a mobile cookbook app developed by Brady Stover (“we,” “us”), handles information. It is written to match how the app works today. If that changes — for example if AI helpers are turned on, or if a sync service is added — this policy will be updated before those features go live.

A short version

Recipe Box is built to stay on your phone. Your recipes, photos, kitchen list, grocery list, meal plan, family names, and cooking history are stored on your device. We do not have a user account system, and we do not run a Recipe Box server that stores your box. We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not use your data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who this policy is for

This policy applies to the iPhone app named Recipe Box. It is written for the App Store listing and for anyone who uses the app.

Information stored on your device

When you use Recipe Box, the following may be saved locally on your iPhone:

  • Recipes you add, including titles, ingredients, steps, notes, tags, a “from” name, a short family story, and photos you attach
  • Photos of handwritten recipe cards that you choose to keep with a recipe
  • Cooking history and ratings, including optional per-person tastes if you add family names
  • Your My Kitchen list (pots, pans, and tools)
  • Your grocery list and Plan my week preferences and the current week’s plan
  • Settings such as a temporary “eat at” time for Ready by (kept for the current session)

This information stays on the device. Deleting a recipe deletes its cook history. Deleting the app deletes the local box, unless you have shared a file with someone else or saved a photo elsewhere yourself.

We do not receive a copy of this on-device information.

Camera and photo library

If you choose to take a picture or pick one from your library — to illustrate a recipe, to photograph a handwritten card, or to photograph ingredients — the app uses the camera or photo library. iOS will ask your permission first. The purpose strings in the app explain this.

Photos you add are stored on your device with the recipe. We do not upload them to a Recipe Box server. The app does not need microphone access.

Information that leaves the device

Some features make network requests. These are not used to build a profile about you.

  • Recipe photos in Discover and some catalog pictures load from Wikimedia Commons, a public media library. The app requests the image file so it can show it, and may cache it on the device. Those requests go to Wikimedia, not to a Recipe Box account.
  • Paste a link (import a recipe from a website you paste) sends a request to the address you entered, so the app can read a recipe from that page. You initiate this. Imported recipes are saved on your device for your personal use, with a link back to the original site.
  • Share with family uses the iOS share sheet to send a recipe or week plan as a message or file from your phone. Nothing is uploaded to a Recipe Box server. The person you share with would have that copy on their device if they import it.
  • Apple In-App Purchase, when Plus is offered for sale, is processed by Apple. We do not see your card number. Until Plus is actually for sale on the App Store, regular App Store builds do not charge you. A development build may complete a sandbox (test) purchase after the product exists, which is not a real charge.

AI helpers (not on by default)

The app includes screens for photographing a fridge or pantry, and for scanning a handwritten recipe card. These helpers are not connected to a live AI service in the version described by this policy. If they are not set up, the app says so and offers a sample so you can try the rest of the flow. Nothing is sent to an AI provider in that state.

If a later version turns those helpers on, they would only run when you ask, and only after you review the result before anything is saved to your box. In that case, the photo or text you submit would be sent to a developer-operated proxy and then to the AI provider the developer has configured, solely to suggest a recipe. This policy would be updated before that happens, and the App Store privacy answers would be updated to say that User Content may be sent to that provider. We will not add a paid AI backend without the developer’s approval.

Purchases and Recipe Box Plus

Recipe Box Plus is planned as a $2.99 per year auto-renewing subscription, with no free trial, sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. The free app is meant to keep your box, Discover, My Kitchen, cook mode, Plan my week, history, and a limited number of link imports. Plus is planned to unlock unlimited link imports and, only once AI helpers are actually available, additional fridge-photo and recipe-card scans.

Plus is not yet for sale on the App Store. A test (sandbox) purchase may be available in a development build after the App Store Connect product exists; Expo Go and regular App Store builds cannot charge you, and real charges stay off until a production release is approved. When Plus is for sale, payment, billing, and cancellation are handled by Apple under your Apple ID. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Restore purchases is available in the app.

Tracking, advertising, and sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use third-party advertising networks in the app. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. We do not collect analytics profiles about you.

Children

Recipe Box is a family cookbook for adults. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Accounts and deletion

There is no Recipe Box account to create, and therefore no account to delete. To remove data from this phone, delete individual recipes in the app, or delete the app. Shared files you already sent to someone else are under their control.

Legal bases and your rights

Because we do not operate a server that holds your box, most requests about “what data you have about me” will be: we do not hold your recipes. You can access, correct, or delete what is on your device in the app itself. If you have a privacy question, email Siudingo223@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and the copy in the app and on the public policy page. Meaningful changes — especially new sharing of data off the device — will be made before that feature ships.
