## What the app is

Recipe Box is a local cookbook: your recipes, photos, kitchen, grocery list, meal plan, and cooking history stay on your device. There is no Recipe Box account.

The Discover library is made of recipes from the [Wikibooks Cookbook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Recipes), licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, and photos from Wikimedia Commons under the license shown on each photo. Those licenses stay with that content. Imported recipes from a website you paste are for your personal use and keep a link to the original page.

You own the recipes and photos you add yourself.
